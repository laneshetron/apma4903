Timing-Aware Prediction Market Maker

A same-path simulation comparing a diffusion-only market maker with a timing-aware controller that anticipates one-shot scheduled information, unscheduled jumps, and stale-quote adverse selection.

The original controller assumes diffusion-only risk. The timing-aware controller knows the scheduled-event hazard curve, accounts for unscheduled jumps, and changes policy after the anticipated information event actually occurs.

What this solver is doing

The state grid is (t,p,q,S), where S=0 means the anticipated event has not yet occurred and S=1 means it has. The solver works backward over 64 time steps, 25 probability states, and 17 inventory states.

At each state the market maker chooses bid and ask offsets from a discrete action grid. Ordinary fills arrive with exponentially declining intensity as quotes move away from fair value. The timing-aware Bellman recursion additionally integrates a one-shot scheduled-event hazard, unscheduled jumps, quadratic inventory risk from jump variance, and stale-quote execution whenever the post-news fair value crosses a resting quote.

Both policies are then evaluated on the same realized probability path and common random order-flow draws. The original controller re-optimizes after a jump but does not anticipate jumps before they occur.

This is a finite-state numerical approximation to the proposed HJB/PIDE system, not a convergence-tested production implementation.

Suggested next extension: add a matched-total-variance diffusion controller to isolate the value of discontinuity and timing information from variance alone.
